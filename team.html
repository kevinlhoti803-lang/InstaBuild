Founder & CEO

Mentor

Visionary Leader · Industry Pioneer
"Building isn't just about structures — it's about creating the spaces where life happens. Every home we build carries that responsibility."

Mentor founded InstaBuild in 2010 with nothing but a bold vision and an unshakable belief that modular construction could change the industry. Under his leadership, the company has grown from a small 5-person team in Prishtina to an internationally recognized firm operating across 9 countries with over 200 employees.

His hands-on approach, strategic foresight, and commitment to quality have earned InstaBuild a reputation as one of Europe's most trusted modular construction companies. Mentor personally reviews every major project, ensuring the founding principles of transparency, speed, and craftsmanship are never compromised. His leadership style — empowering his team while maintaining the highest standards — has created a culture of excellence that defines InstaBuild at every level.
